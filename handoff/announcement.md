Welcome to Automathon 2.0. Your workflows are checked by VeryJudgyCat. Here is all you need.

**Log in** at `<SITE>` with your team code and the password on your card.

**Build.** Your problem statement lists the Webhook names your workflow must have, with the exact request and reply format. The names must match exactly, the method is POST, and your answer goes back through a Respond to Webhook node. Press **Download starter** on the page: it gives you the Webhook nodes already set up, so you only connect them to your own steps. Everything else in your workflow is up to you.

**Upload** your exported workflow. The page checks it straight away and tells you what is missing.

**AI.** Use any AI model you like. Enter your own key on the run form.

**Run.** Free sample runs are unlimited. You get **three scored attempts per round, and your best one counts.**

If a message says **Model Unavailable**, switch to another model. **Key Rate-Limited** means wait a minute and run again. **Refunded** means the problem was on our side and the attempt does not count.

Need help? Raise your hand. Good luck!
